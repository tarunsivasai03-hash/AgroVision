    For a good photo

    • One leaf, filling most of the frame
    • Daylight, without harsh shadows
    • The spotted or discoloured side facing the camera
    • A plain background, such as your palm or a sheet of paper

    Paddy, wheat, cotton, sugarcane and most other crops are not covered. {{ single_class_crops|join(', ')|capitalize }} are not covered either: the scan has seen too few examples to tell healthy leaves from sick ones.

    How far to trust it

    The scan learned from photos taken in a lab. On photos taken in a field it is often wrong, so it only answers when its answer agrees with what the photo shows. Expect it to say it is not sure quite often.
