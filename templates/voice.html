Example call

Press "Play example call" to start.

  1. Dial the number

    From any mobile or landline. No internet needed.

  2. Choose your language

    Press the number for your language when asked.

  3. Describe the problem

    Say the crop and what you see, for example "brown spots on paddy leaves".

  4. Hear what to do

    You get the likely cause and the next steps. Ask again if anything is unclear.
