How a leaf scan works

  1. Take a photo

    One leaf, in daylight, filling most of the frame. Plain background if you can.

  2. Upload it

    The scan checks that it is a leaf of a crop it knows, then compares it with {{ condition_count }} known conditions.

  3. Follow the steps

    If the scan can tell, you get treatment and prevention advice. If not, it says why. Always confirm with your agriculture officer.
